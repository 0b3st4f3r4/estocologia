# Which results of the book are significant for the theory

Triage made on 2026-10-16 over the 39 results raised in
`resultados-que-dependem-de-python.md`. The question here is another one. Instead of "where does the number come from", it wants
to know **what, if it fell, would bring the theory down?** The answer separates three layers: the one that supports an axiom, the
one that confirms a known principle with a number measured in this program, and the one that serves as a didactic illustration.

## Layer A — what the theory cannot lose

| Result | What it supports |
| --- | --- |
| **LaLonde: matching hits the number (+9.6 pp against +11.1 pp) and is rejected in the 1975 placebo (−12.3 pp)** | Severity is not aesthetics: accuracy without power to err is not evidence. It is the result that gives content to $\Phi_3$ and to panel $A_4$. |
| **Danish fires: the insurer's claim falls and the agent's own model falls too (Brier skill −0.015, world growing 4.6% per year)** | The gate judges the **agent**, not only the claim. Without this, "rejecting one's own model" would be rhetoric; with it, it is the mechanism of $\Phi_4$. |
| **Independent unit in four scales: day against patient (0.51 against 0.07 min), 2.65 million calls that are worth 81 days, 1,134 cohorts that are worth 5.8 blocks, 11,598 students that are worth 80 schools** | It is the operational axiom of the program: a margin computed on the wrong unit is a false margin. Four different domains, the same conclusion — that is generality, not example. |
| **Non-ergodicity with an absorbing state: median 2.10× against mean 19.1×, ruin from $L = 4{,}42$, Peters's coin** | The ensemble mean stops being a legitimate summary when the process is multiplicative and has absorption. It brings down the naive use of expected value in decision. |
| **Wild tail with a clean window: $\hat\alpha = 1{,}81$, 36 clean months are worth 2.2 bits, the monthly chance remains 1/38** | Silence is not evidence under a power-law tail. It is the foundation of the rejected $H_1$ and of the treatment of rare events. |
| **The tie in the blind pilot of 12 cases** | It delimits the thesis: in the classic catalogue of errors, the architecture ties with a careful analyst. A negative result that defines scope is theorizing — without it, the thesis would be left without a falsifier. |
| **ICP: the three successes (light tunnel, hERG, yeast) and the fall of STAR when the grouping is corrected** | Cause is what survives the exchange of environment, and the grouping decides the verdict. It is the operational definition of causality that the program adopts — and the second test of the independent unit. |
| **Goodhart measured: 0.145, four times less than the declared 0.6** | Performativity is a magnitude, not an excuse: it is possible to measure how much the forecast deforms the world it forecasts. |
| **Calibration against resolution: ECE 0.0048 with a modest skill of +0.019** | Calibration is fixed, resolution is not. It separates what is fixable from what is a limit of the data — the basis of the decomposition of Murphy used in the book. |

## What the triage changes in the plan

- The invariants from $\Phi_1$ to $\Phi_4$ now have a declared foundation. $\Phi_2$ has four scales, $\Phi_3$
  has LaLonde, $\Phi_4$ has the fires.
- Two theoretical entries were born from the research of this program and not from the literature: **the gate that rejects the
  agent** (Layer A, fires) and **performativity as a magnitude** (Layer A, Goodhart).
- One negative result (the tie of the 12 cases) and one ethical limit (no affected group heard) stay in the body
  of the theory, because they delimit how far it promises.
