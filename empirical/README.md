# Background points of the research

The audit of the book listed ten background points of the research — things that the text asserted or assumed and that had never
been tested. Each point gained a front in this folder, with the same house rule of the horizons: a
`DECLARACAO.md` with hash, recorded **before** running, a `README.md` with the result, and nothing adjusted after seeing
the result. Big data and heavy traces stay outside Git, with `SOURCES.md` and `SHA256SUMS` in the source folder (see
[FORA_DO_GIT.md](../FORA_DO_GIT.md)); the reproduction environment is in [`ENVIRONMENT.md`](ENVIRONMENT.md).

## What is missing

- **The ten fronts and the two machine extensions are closed**, with the qualifications in the README of each one. The two
  derivatives of data have a hash different from the recorded one because of the writer (content checked; see
  `FORA_DO_GIT.reconstruido.sha256`), and the reproduction environment is in [`ENVIRONMENT.md`](ENVIRONMENT.md) and
  [`requirements.txt`](requirements.txt) — where `statsmodels` (used only by the answer key of p01) and `pyreadr` (one step of
  p08) appear as absent, and for that reason those two steps do not run here.
- **It depends on people, and only on that:** the listening to those who pay for the error ([p13-escuta](p13-escuta/README.md): protocol
  frozen, box sealed, 0 of 7 groups heard, deadline on 2026-12-15) and the full panel of SHELF
  ([p12-shelf](p12-shelf/README.md): the track of the first expert is closed in five domains, the
  other two people per domain and the expert from outside the program are missing). The rereading of the 21 opinions came out in
  [`governance/RELEITURA.md`](../governance/RELEITURA.md): 17 confirmed, 4 withdrawn and reissued with a signed note.
- **Divergence of G4 of H3, resolved: it was the numpy of the path.** The published G4 (`h3/saida/portoes.json`: r̂ 0.5632 with 2375 shocks) **reproduces** — in numpy **2.5.1** of `pyenv`, which only enters `sys.path` with `PYTHONNOUSERSITE=1`. With the standard `python3`, which imports numpy **2.4.6** from `~/.local`, the same code gives r̂ 0.5529 with 2395 shocks. The MRE: with the **same** state of PCG64, `binomial(248, 0.6)` returns **171** in 2.4.6 and **150** in 2.5.1 — numpy 2.5 fixed the Stirling series of the acceptance test of BTPE (the 3rd and 4th terms were summed, and the leading coefficient said `13680` instead of `13860`). It was not an open point for long and **no published number changed**: diagnosis and proof in `h3/DECLARACAO_G4_REGENERACAO_2.md`.
- **Taking the summary to the book, done for the ten original fronts.** The points are in section 8 of the article and in section 9.7
  of the textbook; the extensions (health, rare event, card, contract) enter the same section when they close.

## House rules

- The declaration comes first, with hash (`DECLARACAO.sha256`) and time.
- The README of each front says what was done, the numbers, the limits, and how to reproduce.
- Changing a criterion after seeing a result is not forbidden: it enters as a new declaration and counts in the K.
- Failure of execution and deviation of design are reported as a result, not hidden.
