# Opinion — Case 07: sleep app by sex

## Reasoning error
"Significant in women, not significant in men" does not demonstrate that the effect differs between the sexes. For that it is necessary to test the difference between the effects, that is, the interaction.

## Results
- Women: +17.5 min/night (95% CI from 3.9 to 31.1; p = 0.012). Men: +8.2 min (CI from −8.2 to +24.6; p = 0.32). The numbers are reproduced.
- The two CIs overlap widely, and the estimated effect in men is positive. The CI of the men is compatible with effects equal to that of the women.
- Interaction app × sex (OLS): difference of −9.3 min in men relative to women, 95% CI from −30.4 to +11.9, p = 0.39. There is no evidence that the effect is different.
- Model without interaction, the most adequate analysis given the design stratified by sex: common effect of +12.9 min (CI from 2.3 to 23.4; p = 0.017).
- With 60 per arm in each sex, the study has low power to detect an interaction of that size. The absence of significance in men is lack of precision, not evidence of absence of effect.

## Implication for the decision
There is no basis for repositioning the product only for women nor for abandoning men. What the data indicate is a modest effect (~13 min/night) in the set, with no difference demonstrated by sex.
