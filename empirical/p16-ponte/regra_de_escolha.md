# Rule of mechanical choice of the cases (point 16)

**Frozen on 2026-10-16, before a list exists.** Whoever chooses is nobody: the choice is a function of (a) an external
list, (b) the eligibility filter, and (c) the declared seed. Whoever applies the function is the instrument, and the
result comes out before an analyst exists.

## 1. The external list

An external catalog of published cases, with identifier and address, fixed and with a hash recorded **before** the
choice. Accepted source: public catalogs of replication failure and of published correction — the replication project
of the Open Science Collaboration, the registered replication records, and the corrections and retractions of public
catalog. **The author of the program does not touch the list:** any edit of his contaminates exogeneity and invalidates the
test. The list enters with a hash; if the list changes afterwards, the test is another one and the declaration is another one.

## 2. The eligibility filter, objective and declared beforehand

- **Case with a defect:** the catalog documents an error of statistical judgment that passed through peer review —
  replication failure, published correction, or retraction — and the error is **not** declared fraud.
- **Clean case:** the catalog documents successful replication or a result confirmed independently.

The filter uses nothing of the program: neither the list of Makin and Orban de Xivry of 2019, nor the chapters, nor the
domains of the repository. A case only enters if the external catalog classifies it, and the classification stays cited in the
card of the case.

## 3. The draw

With the filtered list fixed and with a hash, the choice is the permutation of the list by the seed and the first ones of the drawn
order, in the declared proportion: **8 cases with a defect and 4 clean**, drawn within each group. Seed
declared and recorded beforehand: **20270114**. The instrument applies the function (`labs --ponte`), and the result is the
file of the 12 cases.

## 4. The order says nothing

The shuffle is the same draw, and the numbering of the cases comes out of it. The number of the case does not inform whether there is a defect,
as in the pilot.

## 5. What invalidates the test

A list edited after the choice; a case swapped by hand; the author seeing the answer key before the analysts; an analyst reading
the answer key or the other cases; a list that does not sustain the requested proportion (for example, fewer than 8 eligible cases with
a defect) — in that case the instrument **rejects** the choice instead of completing it with whatever there is.

## 6. Change of criterion

A change of source, filter, seed, or proportion is a **new declaration**, with its own hash, and counts in the K of the program.
