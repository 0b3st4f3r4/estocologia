## What is checked

- `multiverso`: `specifications=N; agree=A; shares=name:share, name:share`, with N ≥ 2, A ≤ N, and the shares closing
  to one within 0.05, the tolerance of the budget of 1.3.
- `confiabilidade`: `k=K; pass1=P1; passk=PK`, with K ≥ 2 and 0 ≤ PK ≤ P1 ≤ 1, because the chance that K runs all
  succeed cannot exceed the chance that one does.
- **The one rule that ties two fields:** a budget of 1.3 that names a share `specification` is refused when the card
  declares no multiverse, because that share is measured there and nowhere else.

No gate demands the fields, for the reason of declaration 2: demanding them would fail retroactively every card already
approved.
