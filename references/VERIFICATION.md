## Status of the corrections in the repository files (2026-10-15)

Applied in `paper/README.md` on this date: the 1748 title of Hume (it was cited as *An Enquiry*, which only appears in
1758), the rate of predictive testing for Huntington's disease (it was "less than 10%", it became 17.4%, Baig and colleagues 2016),
the attribution of Meyer, Viscusi & Durbin (#14), and the caveat that the classical works were cited from memory,
which was dropped. They remain as corrections to apply wherever the textbook text repeats the citation: #17 (Baltagi and
Levin came out in *Structural Change and Economic Dynamics*, not in *Empirical Economics*), #18 (indicate the edition of
Stock & Watson), #24 (SHELF is not a synonym of the book by O'Hagan and colleagues) and the addition of volume, number, pages,
and DOI of #2, #3, #5, #7, #19, and #22. In the repository, the two files that still repeat #17 and #18 with the tag "cited from memory, check" are `data/h2b/FONTES.md` and `data/h2e/FONTES.md`; the textbook repeats neither of the two.

- **#14:** replace "Viscusi & Durbin (1995)" with **Meyer, Viscusi & Durbin (1995)**. The first author, Bruce D. Meyer, was missing. Full reference: *American Economic Review* 85(3):322–340.
- **#17:** the article by Baltagi & Levin (1992) **did not come out in *Empirical Economics***. It came out in ***Structural Change and Economic Dynamics*** 3(2):321–335, DOI 10.1016/0954-349X(92)90010-4.   - **#24:** do not treat SHELF and the book *Uncertain Judgements* as the same work.
